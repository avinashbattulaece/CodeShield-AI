# CodeShield AI Presentation Content

## Slide 1 - Title
CodeShield AI
Intelligent Source Code Plagiarism Detection System

## Slide 2 - Problem
Manual plagiarism checking is slow and difficult for large classes.

## Slide 3 - Objective
Automatically compare submissions and identify suspicious similarities.

## Slide 4 - Key Features
ZIP upload, multi-file comparison, risk classification, dashboard, charts, PDF/CSV reports.

## Slide 5 - Methodology
Upload -> Safe Extraction -> Preprocessing -> AST Normalization -> Structural Analysis -> Similarity Score -> Risk Classification.

## Slide 6 - Accuracy Improvement
Variable/function names are normalized while operators and program structure are preserved.

## Slide 7 - False Positive Reduction
Addition and multiplication are treated differently because AST operator information is retained.

## Slide 8 - Dashboard
Displays total comparisons and High/Medium/Low risk analytics.

## Slide 9 - Reports
Results can be downloaded as PDF and CSV.

## Slide 10 - Future Scope
Cross-language AST analysis, ML-based similarity, LMS integration, authentication and cloud deployment.
